Models and settings

Player

Characters

Each character has their own persona and sees only what they were present for. The director chooses who answers. Status, reading and relationship are kept current by the watcher; correct them here when they drift.

Narrator

An optional extra voice for description and transitions. Off by default; the director picks it only when the scene needs it.

Places

Define the world's locations the way you define the cast. Whoever is at the scene's place is told its description and what is nearby. Each character is in a place (set on the Cast tab), and who is present is simply who is where the scene is. The watcher moves the scene and people between these places and keeps each one's condition current; it never invents a place. Connections say what is nearby, they don't forbid going anywhere else.

Scene

Updated by the watcher each turn. Everyone sees the place and the time; an open thread is shown only to those who were present when it arose.

Scene presets

A ready-made starting point: a place, the time, who is where and doing what, what is hanging, and an optional premise and opening line. Starting one ends the current scene and sets the new one up. The cast, places and memories carry on.
