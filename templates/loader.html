Sessions

A session is a saved playthrough: the world as it stands, the memories and the conversation. Loading one resumes it, and whatever you were playing is kept in the Autosave slot first, so a switch never loses anything.

Scenarios

A scenario is a reusable world with a clean start: no memories and no conversation. Starting one begins a fresh playthrough. Edit one to change it for every playthrough you start from then on; a playthrough already running keeps its own copy. Scenarios are files in scenarios/.

New scenario

It drafts the whole world: places and how they connect, a cast with voices, hidden facts with hints, a couple of scene presets and topics to keep open. It is saved to your scenarios and nothing you are playing changes. Read it before you play it: a model can slip a hidden fact into a description.
