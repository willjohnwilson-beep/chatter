Reserved topics

Lore you are keeping for yourself. Name a topic (for example "who built the Vault") and nobody will invent anything about it: characters are told they don't know and the narrator leaves it unexplained. Write the topic, never the answer. Tag characters or places to tell only them (or anyone in a scene at those places); leave both blank to tell everyone.

Schedule

Things that happen on the story's clock, like a food drop every Wednesday. When the story's date passes a day an event falls on, it happens in front of whoever is there, and they remember it. Write when in plain words: "every Wednesday", "daily", "every other day", "every 3 days", "15 October". Leave the place blank for an event that reaches the scene wherever it is. An event only fires if the story's time has a date or a weekday in it; the watcher keeps that moving.

Knowledge

Facts particular characters know from the very start: what someone was briefed on, what they have noticed, what the whole house knows. Each is kept as a memory for exactly the people you name, so nobody else's prompt ever holds it, and it is checked against what is said in front of people who don't know it. Put what makes someone who they are in their persona; put what they know here. Write a fact about a character in the second person if it is about them ("You are...") and in the third otherwise. "Always in mind" keeps it in every prompt; otherwise it comes back when the topic does.
